## What is CruiseBuddy?

CruiseBuddy brings your sailing dates, ports, excursions, travel documents and pre-cruise tasks into one app. Whether you're preparing for your first sailing or keeping several upcoming trips organised, it gives every detail a home so you can spend more time looking forward to your holiday.

Use it with any cruise line: add your ship, cabin and booking details, then build your plans around the way you travel.

## From the first booking to the last port

- **Plan your itinerary.** Keep sailing dates, ports, sea days and shore excursions together.
- **Keep documents handy.** Organise tickets, booking confirmations, insurance and other travel documents.
- **Stay on top of costs.** Track deposits, payments, balances and due dates.
- **Get ready to sail.** Work through packing lists, to-dos and reminders before departure.
- **Bring the whole journey together.** Keep flights, hotels, transfers and travel notes alongside your cruise.
- **Share with your companions.** Send selected trip details through encrypted links that expire automatically.

## Your plans, your privacy

Cruise plans stay on your device by default. When you choose to share a plan, CruiseBuddy encrypts it on your device before uploading it. [Learn how sharing works](https://cruise-buddy.app/sharing).
